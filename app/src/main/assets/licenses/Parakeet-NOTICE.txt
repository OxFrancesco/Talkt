Parakeet CTC 0.6B and TDT 0.6B v3

Original models: NVIDIA
https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-ctc-0.6b
https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3

https://github.com/tetherto/qvac-ext-ggml
