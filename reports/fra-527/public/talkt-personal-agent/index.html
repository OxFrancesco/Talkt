What works

  • Telegram sends prompts and receives real model replies.
  • Approved GitHub and Google Drive calls run on your Mac and return through Telegram.
  • Your signed-in app on the Fold sees the same Telegram conversation.
  • The personal profile loads your global AGENTS.md and skills. The runner reads project instructions and supporting files.

The Mac runner starts at login and restarts on exit. Keep the Mac awake and its SSD mounted for local tools. Other Talkt accounts remain isolated.
